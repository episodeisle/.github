<h3 align="center">An island of short dramas.</h3>

<p align="center">Hold your phone upright, swipe, watch. Every episode runs a few minutes and ends on a moment that makes you want the next one.</p>

---

EpisodeIsle is a site for watching dramas. Our series are shot vertically for a phone screen and told in short episodes, the kind you can fit into a bus ride, a queue, or the last few minutes before sleep. When an episode ends, swipe up and the next one starts right away.

The first episodes of every series are free. You unlock the rest with credits, and you get credits just for logging in each day.

We're launching on the web first. Phone apps will follow.
